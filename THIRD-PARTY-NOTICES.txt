INEGI geographic catalog
-------------------------

The offline entity and municipality/demarcation name list in
src/SistemaDocente.Presentation/Data/estados-municipios.json is an AulaRaíz
transformation of the official INEGI Catálogo Único de Claves de Áreas
Geoestadísticas Estatales, Municipales y Localidades.

Fuente: INEGI, Catálogo Único de Claves de Áreas Geoestadísticas Estatales,
Municipales y Localidades, classification updated through 17 June 2025.

Official catalog:
https://www.inegi.org.mx/app/ageeml/

Official municipal-area service:
https://www.inegi.org.mx/servicios/catalogounico.html

INEGI terms of free use:
https://www.inegi.org.mx/inegi/terminos.html

AulaRaíz retains only the entity and municipality/demarcation names, groups
them by entity and sorts them for offline selection. It omits geographic keys,
population values, geometry and other source attributes. This extraction and
reordering were performed by AulaRaíz, not by INEGI. AulaRaíz is not endorsed,
integrated, sponsored or supported by INEGI.

The reviewed snapshot contains 32 entities and 2,478 municipalities or Mexico
City territorial demarcations. Detailed transformation and update instructions
are recorded in
src/SistemaDocente.Presentation/Data/estados-municipios.SOURCE.md.
